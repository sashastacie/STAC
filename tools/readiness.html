The 9 guidelines

Voice: polished, human, quietly confident

Sounds like STAC

  • "Built to work. Not to impress."
  • "If it doesn't run on Monday morning, it isn't finished."
  • "Come with the chaos. Leave with the clarity."
  • Name the deliverable: "a standalone report on what's working and what's not."
  • Talk to one reader: "your books, your cashflow, your team."

Doesn't sound like STAC

  • "World-class, cutting-edge solutions."
  • "We guarantee results!"
  • "Leveraging synergies for holistic, seamless growth."
  • "We'll try to help where we can, hopefully."
  • Sentences so long the reader has to go back to the start.
