# STAC chatbot: 50 questions & answers (EN / NL)

Approved content for the chatbot. Answers come from the website and from the owner's confirmations (30 Sep 2026).

`\n` in an answer starts a new line in the chat bubble.

Style rules used: short, human, confident but not cocky; Dutch uses formal "u"; no client names; no invented prices or promises;
every answer ends where a real person would, often with a next step.

---
